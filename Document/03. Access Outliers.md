

# 03. Access Outliers

## Reviewing Existing Access

I compared the 12 users' current permissions against the proposed RBAC roles to identify access that may be excessive, inconsistent or difficult to justify.

The proposed roles provide a baseline, but final access decisions require confirmation from the relevant business owners.

## Access Consistent with the Proposed Design


**Ivan • Support Agent**
 currently has View Invoices only. This aligns with the proposed Billing Viewer role, subject to confirming which billing records Ivan is authorised to see.

## Review Priorities

**First — Potential financial control conflicts**

Farid and Hana hold both **Approve Refunds** and **Issue Refunds**. Their access needs prompt review because it could undermine independent refund approval if the application permits the same person to perform both actions on one transaction.

**Second — Sensitive or unusually broad access**

Review Lena's ability to edit billing records, Hana's administrative and deletion permissions, and Farid's deletion and export rights.

**Third — Role inconsistencies and business justification**

Review Chloe, Jo, Kai, Ana, Ben, Dev, Elena and Grace against their actual responsibilities. Some differences may be legitimate exceptions rather than permissions that should automatically be removed.

## Recommended Approach

For each flagged permission, I would:

1. Confirm the employee's responsibilities with the business owner.
2. Identify the appropriate proposed role or approved exception.
3. Remove or restrict permissions that cannot be justified.
4. Verify that the employee can still complete authorised work.

**The goal is to correct unnecessary access without treating every difference in the matrix as a security violation.**

*This is a proposed access review. No permissions have been changed.*
