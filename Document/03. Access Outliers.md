## Access Consistent with the Proposed Design

**Ivan (Support Agent)**<BR>Currently has View Invoices only. This aligns with the proposed Billing Viewer role, subject to confirming which billing records Ivan is authorised to see.

<BR>

## Review Priorities

### 1. Potential Financial Control Conflicts

**Farid and Hana** hold both refund approval and issuance permissions, creating a potential SoD conflict.

**Dev, Elena and Grace** can prepare invoices and approve refunds. This combination also requires review to establish whether independent approval controls are necessary.

### 2. Sensitive or Unusually Broad Access

Review Lena's invoice editing, Hana's administrative and deletion permissions, and Farid's deletion and export access.

### 3. Role Inconsistencies Requiring Justification

Review the remaining users against their actual responsibilities. Differences may be legitimate exceptions rather than permissions that should automatically be removed.

<BR>

## Recommended Approach

- Validate flagged permissions against job responsibilities, approve justified exceptions and remove unsupported access.

- Verify that employees can still perform their authorised duties after changes.
