
# 05. Rollout Plan

**Step 1:  Confirm the roles**  
Check the proposed roles with managers and team leads. Note any specialist responsibilities before we remove access.

**Step 2: Run in parallel**  
Enable the new roles alongside current permissions for two weeks. Watch the logs to see what is actually being used.

**Step 3: Fix highest risk first**  
Start with Farid and Hana — their conflicting permissions create the greatest risk. Then resolve Jo, Lena, Chloe and Kai. Replace Delete Invoice with a break-glass process that requires approval and logging.

**Step 4: Communicate and protect**  
Tell affected staff what is changing. Keep a rollback plan ready. Schedule access reviews every quarter.

**Go/No-Go:** Complete the migration only when Finance and Billing confirm essential tasks still work and the security controls are in place.
