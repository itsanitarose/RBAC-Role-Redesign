## Proposed Rollout Plan

The transition to RBAC follows five phases, reducing access risks while protecting Northwind Health's billing operations.

**1. Validate and Approve**  
Confirm the six roles, user assignments and justified exceptions with Finance, Billing and IT. Review Farid and Hana's conflicting refund permissions immediately, contain any confirmed unsafe access and resolve outstanding questions around invoice deletion and sensitive access.

**2. Pilot Read-Only Access**  
Start with Ivan and other approved read-only users. Verify that the Billing Viewer role supports their required tasks before expanding the rollout.

**3. Migrate Transactional Roles**  
Introduce invoice, refund, export and administrative roles in stages, avoiding critical billing periods.

**4. Remove Legacy Permissions**  
Remove unnecessary individual grants only after replacement role access is tested and confirmed.

**5. Verify and Monitor**  
Test billing workflows, SoD controls and sensitive access restrictions. Monitor failures and maintain a tested rollback procedure.

**Go/No-Go:** Complete migration only when Finance, Billing and IT approve the changes, essential tasks work and required security controls pass verification.
