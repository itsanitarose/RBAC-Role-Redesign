# 02. Role Catalogue

## Proposed RBAC Design

Northwind Health is growing, but its current approach of copying permissions between employees is difficult to maintain and risks carrying unnecessary access forward.

A clear, maintainable and scalable access control structure is needed.

I propose six reusable roles using **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)**. Each role has defined permissions based on job responsibilities, allowing employees to receive consistent access without inheriting someone else's permissions.

## Proposed Role Catalogue

| Role | Permissions | Intended Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Billing Viewer** | View Invoices | Read-only access for authorised support staff and auditors |
| **Invoice Operator** | View, Create, Edit Invoices | Preparing and maintaining invoices |
| **Refund Processor** | View Invoices, Issue Refunds | Processing approved refunds |
| **Refund Approver** | View Invoices, Approve Refunds | Independently authorising refunds |
| **Billing Data Exporter** | View Invoices, Export Patient Billing Data | Approved reporting and data exports |
| **Billing Administrator** | Admin Settings | Application configuration and maintenance |

<BR>
Proposed permissions reflect the minimum access required for each role, subject to business policy and approval.

<BR>

## Design Decisions

- **Least Privilege:** Permissions are grouped by business function, with additional access requiring justification.
- **Segregation of Duties:** Refund approval and issuance are separated, with controls needed to prevent users from approving their own transactions.
- **Sensitive Access:** Patient data exports and administrative settings are restricted to designated roles.
- **Invoice Deletion:** Excluded from standard roles until a legitimate business requirement is confirmed.

## Designed for Growth

- **Existing employees:** Review current permissions, assign appropriate roles and identify unnecessary access for removal.
- **Future employees:** Assign approved roles during onboarding instead of copying another employee's permissions.

*Proposed design only. Permissions, role combinations and business requirements must be validated before implementation.*
