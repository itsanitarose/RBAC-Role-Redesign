
# 02. Role Catalogue

## Proposed RBAC Design

The existing permissions matrix contains 12 users across five job roles, but employees with the same job title do not always have the same access.

I propose six functional roles based on the activities employees need to perform. A user may hold more than one role where justified, provided the combination does not introduce a segregation-of-duties conflict.

## Proposed Role Catalogue

| Proposed Role | Permissions | Intended Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Billing Viewer** | View Invoices | Read-only access for authorised employees, including support staff and auditors, with visibility limited to the records they need. |
| **Invoice Operator** | View Invoices, Create Invoice, Edit Invoice | Employees responsible for preparing and maintaining invoices. |
| **Refund Processor** | View Invoices, Issue Refunds | Authorised staff responsible for processing approved refunds. |
| **Refund Approver** | View Invoices, Approve Refunds | Designated employees responsible for independently approving refunds. |
| **Billing Data Exporter** | View Invoices, Export Patient Billing Data | Employees with a verified business requirement to export billing information. |
| **Billing Administrator** | Admin Settings | IT personnel responsible for system configuration and maintenance. |

## Design Decisions

### 1. Separate Refund Processing from Approval

**Issue Refunds** and **Approve Refunds** belong to different roles.

Users should not hold both roles for the same billing workflow. The application must also enforce independent approval at the transaction level.

### 2. Restrict Sensitive Data Exports

**Export Patient Billing Data** should not automatically accompany Finance Analyst or Billing Specialist access.

Export rights require a documented business reason, appropriate approval and restrictions on the data that can be exported.

### 3. Separate Administration from Financial Operations

The Billing Administrator role contains **Admin Settings**, without automatically granting access to invoices or financial transactions.

Any additional access needed for troubleshooting should be separately justified and controlled.

### 4. Keep Invoice Deletion Outside Standard Roles

**Delete Invoice** is excluded from the six proposed roles because the source information does not establish who requires it.

If deletion is necessary, the business owner should define an approved process with appropriate authorisation and oversight.

### 5. Apply Access Restrictions Beyond Role Membership

A role defines which actions a user can perform. It does not, by itself, determine which patient or billing records they should see.

Support staff and external auditors may both need read-only access, but their visibility should reflect their different responsibilities. These restrictions must be verified in the application.

## Assumptions Requiring Business Validation

The proposed roles are a starting point, not an approved access policy.

Before assigning them, I would confirm:

- Which employees genuinely create, edit, approve and issue refunds.
- Whether refund approval and processing can be enforced as separate steps.
- Who requires bulk exports of patient billing information.
- Whether invoice deletion is required and how it should be controlled.
- Which records support staff, auditors and other users need to access.

The objective is not to create six new labels for existing permissions. It is to establish a role structure where **each permission has a clear business purpose and sensitive activities remain appropriately separated.**
