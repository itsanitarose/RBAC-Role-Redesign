# 04. Segregation of Duties

**Which permission pairs should never sit with one person, and who currently breaks that rule?**

## Pairs that should never sit with one person

| Permission Pair | Risk |
|-----------------|------|
| **Issue Refunds + Approve Refunds** | Someone could approve their own refunds |
| **Create Invoice or Edit Invoice + Approve Refunds** | Someone could create or change an invoice and then approve a related refund |
| **Create Invoice or Edit Invoice + Delete Invoice** | Someone could create a record and then delete the evidence |
| **Admin Settings + any transactional permission** (Create Invoice, Edit Invoice, Delete Invoice, Issue Refunds or Approve Refunds) | An administrator should not be able to process or change financial transactions |

<br>

## Who currently breaks these rules

- **Farid** holds:
  - Issue Refunds + Approve Refunds
  - Create/Edit Invoice + Approve Refunds
  - Create/Edit Invoice + Delete Invoice

- **Hana** holds:
  - Issue Refunds + Approve Refunds
  - Create/Edit Invoice + Approve Refunds
  - Create/Edit Invoice + Delete Invoice
  - Admin Settings together with financial permissions

These are the highest-priority conflicts to resolve.

<br>

## Recommended Controls

- Assign refund approval and issuance to different people.
- Prevent users from approving refunds they created or issued (transaction level maker-checker).
- Keep system administration separate from financial permissions.
- Review role combinations regularly.

## Verification Required

Confirm the application actually prevents self-approval and that admin access cannot bypass the controls above.
