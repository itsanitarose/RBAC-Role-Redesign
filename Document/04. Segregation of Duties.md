
# 04. Segregation of Duties


<br>

## Proposed SoD Rules

| Permission Combination | Risk | Proposed Control |
|---|---|---|
| **Approve&nbsp;Refunds + Issue&nbsp;Refunds** | One person could authorise and process the same refund. | Separate approval and processing. Prevent self-approval. |
| **Create/Edit Invoice + Approve Refunds** | A user could potentially approve a refund linked to an invoice they prepared or changed. | Require independent approval for transactions involving the user's own work. |
| **Admin Settings + Financial Transactions** | Administrative authority combined with financial permissions could allow controls to be changed or bypassed. | Separate administration from financial duties and review privileged activity. |

The first combination is a proposed strict separation. The other two require validation against the application's actual workflows and controls.

<br>

## Recommended Controls

- **Role Separation:** Assign refund approval and processing to different authorised users.
- **Transaction-Level Enforcement:** Prevent users from approving refunds they initiated or issued.
- **Administrative Separation:** Restrict system administration to authorised IT personnel, with exceptions independently approved.
- **Access Reviews:** Regularly check role combinations and investigate new conflicts.

<br>

## Verification Required

The permission matrix confirms which permissions users hold, but not whether the application enforces independent approval.

Testing must establish whether users can approve their own transactions, bypass approval steps or change controls through administrative access.
