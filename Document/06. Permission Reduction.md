# 06. Permission Reduction

<br>

## Measuring the Impact of RBAC

The original billing matrix contains **43 individual permission grants** across 12 users.

The proposed design introduces six reusable roles containing **11 role-permission assignments**.

<BR>

## Before and After RBAC

| Measurement | Current Design | Proposed Design |
|---|---:|---:|
| Users | 12 | 12 |
| Individual permission grants | 43 | Replaced by approved role assignments |
| Reusable roles | Not defined | 6 |
| Role-permission assignments | Not applicable | 11 |

<BR>

## How Many Grants Disappear?

**Up to 43 direct permission grants could be retired** if all access is migrated to approved roles without retaining individual grants.

However, this does not mean 43 permissions are unnecessary or that every employee will need fewer permissions.

The proposed role catalogue covers seven of the eight existing permission types. **Delete Invoice remains unresolved**, affecting Farid and Hana.

The final design must establish:

- Which roles each employee requires.
- Which existing permissions are unnecessary.
- Whether justified exceptions are needed.
- How invoice deletion should be controlled.

<BR>

## Assessment

RBAC reduces the need to manage permissions individually by assigning access through reusable roles.

**The real improvement is not simply fewer permissions. It is making access easier to assign, justify, review and revoke.**
