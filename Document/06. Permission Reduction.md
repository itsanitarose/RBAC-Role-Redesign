# 06. Permission Grant Reduction

This assessment measures how many individual permission grants could be retired, not how many permissions employees should lose.

<BR>

## Current vs Proposed Design

| **Measurement** | **Current** | **Proposed** |
|---|---|---|
| Users | 12 | 12 |
| Direct user permission grants | 43 | 0 (conditional) |
| Reusable roles | Not defined | 6 |
| Role permission assignments | Not applicable | 11 |

*Assumes the billing application supports role based access without retaining direct permission grants. This capability has not been verified.*

<BR>

## Calculation

**43 current direct grants − 0 proposed direct grants = 43 grants retired.**

Under a fully role based design, all 43 existing direct grants could be replaced by access through roles.

However, this is an administrative reduction, not evidence that 43 permissions are unnecessary.

<BR>

## Outstanding Decisions

Before migration, the proposed role assignments must be validated against actual responsibilities.

In particular:

- **Farid and Hana:** Resolve conflicting refund permissions.
- **Invoice processing:** Confirm whether employees need multiple functional roles.
- **Sensitive access:** Validate export and administrative requirements.
- **Invoice deletion:** Determine how this permission should be authorised and controlled.

The final number depends on whether any approved exceptions require direct permission grants.

<BR>

## Conclusion

**Potential reduction: all 43 direct permission grants, replaced by access through roles. Final count subject to validation.**

RBAC reduces individually managed access through reusable roles. Employees retain permissions justified by their responsibilities, while unnecessary access is addressed separately.
